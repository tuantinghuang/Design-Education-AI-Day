About

In response to the impact and challenges that AI brings to design classrooms, rather than large-format talks, we want to shape a day that leaves more room for direct conversation and tangible practice. The programme centres on guided discussions, hands-on workshops, and moments of collaborative co-creation, organised around experiences that educators are navigating right now.

Topics include AI acknowledgement in student work, assignment and course design, students' critical thinking, and co-creating principles to bring back to your own teaching context. We hope every participant leaves with something concrete and useful.

The spirit of this series of programmes isn't aimed at AI experts or those with strong technical backgrounds — we'd like to include design educators who are simply encountering AI in their teaching, in whatever form that takes. We find that honest, generative conversation tends to come from that shared experience, and we hope it can be the start of a community that navigates the uncertainty AI brings to design education together.

The event is part of a longer research thread. With the support by the Industrial Design Department at Eindhoven University of Technology.
