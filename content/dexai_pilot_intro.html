Design Education x AI Pilot

In response to the impact and challenges that AI brings to design classrooms, we wanted to organize a day that leaves more room for direct conversation and tangible practice, rather than talks and keynotes. The programme centres on guided discussions, hands-on workshops, and moments of collaborative co-creation, organised around experiences that educators are navigating right now. We hope every participant leaves with something concrete and useful.

The spirit of this gathering isn't aimed at AI experts or those with strong technical backgrounds; we’d like to include design educators who are simply encountering AI in their teaching, in whatever form that takes. We find that honest, generative conversation tends to come from shared experience, and we hope it can be the start of a community that navigates the uncertainty AI brings to design education together.
