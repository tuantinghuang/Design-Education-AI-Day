Design Education x AI Pilot

Reflection

We see many around us focus on how AI is disrupting our way of working and teaching. At the same time, we see the many benefits this new technology brings, as well as the attention we need to give to how we train new designers in their relationship with AI.

In this pilot event, we opened the floor for people to contest, critique, and re-imagine how we can shape the future of education. After the positive reception of the day, we will put our efforts towards turning this pilot into a series of recurring events, each focusing on more specific facets of design education, while keeping our goals of facilitating hands-on activities where educators can walk out with something to take to the classroom.
