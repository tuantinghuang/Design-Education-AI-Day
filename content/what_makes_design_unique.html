Activity Format

In this session, we conducted in breakout rooms with different topics but similar focus. Both rooms feed back into the question of What makes design discipline unique and resilient to AI presence? In one room, we discussed the topic around positive AI stories and anecdotes; in the other room, we discussed students’ AI use and teachers’ response.

The breakout room session was 45 minutes, followed by a 30-minute plenary discussion around the core question.

Outcomes

Positive AI stories and anecdotes

  • Discussions around what makes a good AI project and what makes a good example?
    • Many discussion points revolved around examples in design work and how designers use examples not as informational but as inspirational.
    • GenAI creates “design-shaped things” that can give the false impression of control in the design process. They can also serve as too High Fidelity to serve a design process.
    • Good examples of AI use in student projects is correlated with students making intentional decisions on “AI fit” (i.e., does it make sense to use AI here, in this part of my process?)
