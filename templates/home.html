In response to the impact and challenges that AI brings to design classrooms, we wanted to organize a day that leaves more room for direct conversation and tangible practice, rather than talks and keynotes. The programme centres on guided discussions, hands-on workshops, and moments of collaborative co-creation, organised around experiences that educators are navigating right now.
